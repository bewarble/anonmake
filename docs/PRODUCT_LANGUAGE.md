# AnonMake Product Language

## Public offer

The only public wording for access pricing is:

**1 ₽ — 1 day of access.**

Users must not see:

- renewal amounts;
- internal billing stages or fallback logic;
- the words trial, primary, fallback, MIT or binding;
- technical payment codes;
- paid-through dates, access expiration dates or next-charge dates.

Those details remain available only to administrators.

## Message structure

Use one semantic emoji at the start:

- ✅ success
- ⚠️ warning
- ❌ error
- ℹ️ neutral information
- 💌 anonymous message
- 👤 sender or user
- ⭐ access
- 🔗 personal link
- ✍️ text input
- ⚙️ settings

Keep messages short. Prefer one title and one supporting sentence.

## Voice

- calm;
- direct;
- friendly;
- no technical jargon;
- no duplicated explanation;
- no exaggerated marketing language.

## Examples

```text
✅ Сообщение отправлено.
```

```text
⭐ Доступ

1 ₽ — 1 день доступа.
```

```text
⚙️ Отключить автопродление?

Доступ останется активным.
```
